This price disclosure is an example based on average prices for Residential meter customers only, and does not
necessarily reflect your actual TDU charges as a non-Residential meter customer. Your actual average price for electric
service will vary according to your usage. The price you pay each month will consist of the Base Charge, Energy
Charge, and TDU Delivery Charges. TDU Delivery Charges include all recurring charges from your TDU passed
through to you without mark-up.
Each month your bill will also include (i) all applicable taxes, including sales tax and reimbursement for the state
miscellaneous gross receipts tax, (ii) TDU fees for underground service (if applicable at your location), (iii) facility
relocation fees or other charges ordered by a municipality. The average prices listed above do not include any of the
charges listed in (i), (ii) or (iii).
