The average prices disclosed above reflect a $5.00 monthly credit for enrollment in autopay. All new accounts will automatically receive this $5.00 credit on their initial invoice as a courtesy grace period. To maintain this credit on subsequent invoices, a valid credit card or debit card must be registered for autopay via the Customer Portal prior to the issuance date of your second monthly invoice. If valid card enrollment is not confirmed by that date, the $5.00 credit will be removed from your second and all subsequent invoices until valid card enrollment is successfully completed and remains active with sufficient funds to process recurring payments. Due to extended verification timelines, bank account routing (ACH) registration is not supported for initial setup.

This price disclosure is an example based on the usage levels shown above. Your average price for electricity will depend on your exact monthly usage. The price you pay each month will consist of the applicable Octopus Energy charge, base charge, and the TDU recurring charges (per meter and per kWh). TDU charges will be itemised separately on your bill. This price is fixed for the term of the contract except as noted below.

Each month you will also be billed all taxes, including sales tax, and reimbursement for the state miscellaneous gross receipts tax, as applicable. These taxes are not included in the above average price calculation.

The average prices listed above do not include other Octopus Energy fees and TDU fees for underground service (if applicable to your location) or facility relocation fees or other charges ordered by a municipality.
